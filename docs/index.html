Enter a patent number. Get a prior-art workup.

The page pulls the patent's claims, splits them into separate limitations, and searches scholarly publications that predate the priority date for each one. You review the results; nothing is counted as evidence until you confirm it.

Research aid, not legal advice. Search hits are leads, not proof of prior art. Confirm every document, date and passage in the original source, and have qualified patent counsel review what you rely on.

Setup: data relay and manual fallback

Browsers can't read Google Patents directly, so the page uses a free relay you deploy once (code in worker/worker.js, about two minutes on Cloudflare Workers). Paste its address here; it's saved in this browser.

No relay yet? Paste claim text instead. Put each limitation on its own line, and the page runs the same searches.
